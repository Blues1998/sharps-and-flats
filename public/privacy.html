Sharps&Flats

Privacy Policy

Last updated 17 November 2026

The short version: the Sharps&Flats Android app has no accounts and no ads. Your songs, progress and settings stay on your phone. To fix bugs and see which lessons work, the app sends anonymous usage and crash reports, which you can turn off in Me. The microphone is used live to hear your notes and is never recorded or sent anywhere.

Who we are

Sharps&Flats is an app for learning songs on guitar and ukulele, made by an independent developer. This policy covers the Android app and the website at blues1998.github.io/sharps-and-flats.

The Android app

What stays on your phone. The songs and chord charts you add, your practice history, scores, the tabs you import and your settings are saved in the app's own storage on your device. We cannot see them. They are deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

Microphone. The tuner, voice range and note games listen through the microphone to work out which note you are playing or singing. The sound is analysed live on your phone and thrown away straight after. Nothing is recorded, saved or sent off your device. The app only asks for the microphone when you open a feature that needs it, and you can turn the permission off at any time in Android settings.

Network. All sounds, fonts and pictures are built into the app. Apart from the anonymous reports below, the app sends nothing to us or to anyone else. Your songs and progress only leave your phone if you choose to: for example, by exporting your library as a file and sharing it yourself.

Usage and crash reports. To find bugs and see which lessons and games help people learn, the app sends two kinds of anonymous report:

  • Usage events, to PostHog: things like opening the app, which screen you open, starting or finishing a lesson, a game's score, minutes practised and whether you allowed the microphone.
  • Crash reports, to Sentry: when something goes wrong, the error, the screen it happened on and the last few usage events before it.

Each report carries the app version, your phone's model and Android version, and a random ID made on your phone for this install. None of them include your name, email, the songs or text you add, or any audio. Both services receive your IP address when a report arrives, as any server does, and are set not to store it. You can turn both off at any time in Me, under Your data, Share usage and crash reports.

No ads or ad tracking. There are no ads and no advertising trackers, and nothing is used to build an advertising profile.

The website

The website runs the same app in your browser. It sends no usage or crash reports. It has two additions:

  • Optional account. If you choose to sign in to back up your songs, we store your email address, your songs and your practice history with our database provider, Supabase, so they can sync between browsers. We use this only to provide the backup.
  • Hosting. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may log your IP address when you visit, under GitHub's privacy statement.

Deleting your data

  • Android app: uninstall the app, or go to Android Settings, Apps, Sharps&Flats, Storage, Clear data.
  • Usage and crash reports: these are not linked to your name or email, and Sentry and PostHog delete them automatically after a limited time. Turning the switch off in Me stops new ones.
  • Website account: open the account page, sign in and choose Delete account. Your account and everything backed up to it are deleted immediately.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone. The Android app collects no personal information; its usage and crash reports are anonymous.

Sharing and selling

We do not sell or rent your data. Usage and crash reports go only to Sentry and PostHog, which handle them for us.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. If a change affects what the app does with your data, we will say so in the app's release notes.

Contact
